We Didn't Start as Marketers. That's the Point.

THC Business Solutions was built by people who worked inside healthcare operations rather than inside advertising. That changes what you notice is broken, and in what order you'd fix it.

Our Purpose

Why THC Exists

Most consultancies and agencies serving healthcare organizations are built around what's visible: campaigns, rebrands, training days. They're good at producing those things, but not built to notice that a rebrand is compensating for a scheduling system nobody fixed, or that a training day is standing in for a decision-making problem on the floor. THC Business Solutions exists to catch what other firms miss in that gap. It isn't an add-on to a standard consulting practice. It's the starting premise of the company: look at the operational, cultural, and financial structure first, because that's the order problems compound in, and the order fixes hold in.

Our Method

The Method Behind the Diagnosis

Every engagement, regardless of which of the three verticals it starts in, runs through the same three-part method. It isn't a formality. It's the reason a THC engagement tends to hold after the invoice is paid, where a single-layer fix often doesn't.

Human & Behavioral Diagnostics

Before anything structural gets recommended, we spend time understanding how people behave inside the organization: what a nurse has learned happens when she flags a scheduling conflict, whether a front-desk coordinator has real authority to fix a recurring problem or just absorbs complaints about it, and where the gap sits between what a policy says and what happens on a Tuesday afternoon. Most operational assessments skip this. It takes longer to see than a process diagram.

Analytical & Data-Driven Rigor

In parallel, we run a direct, numbers-based analysis of workflows, costs, staffing, and supply data, built on a proprietary four-layer business analysis framework developed from cross-industry case work and adapted here to healthcare. Its job is to take the pattern the behavioral diagnosis surfaces and confirm it with a number, or correct it.

Business Strategy & Execution

The two diagnostic layers converge into one sequenced roadmap. We mean sequenced literally: a healthcare organization can usually absorb only so much change at once, and the order interventions happen in is often the difference between a fix that holds and one that gets quietly reversed.

Founding

Who's Behind It

THC Business Solutions was founded by a biomedical engineer with a background in healthcare business operations, rather than by a marketing executive or a management consultant by trade. That shapes how the company approaches an engagement: reading a workflow, a piece of clinical equipment, or a procurement contract the way an engineer reads a system, for how the parts interact. It's also why the Procurement vertical is staffed by biomedical engineers and product specialists rather than generalist purchasing agents. Evaluating a piece of medical equipment properly means understanding it technically first, pricing it second.

Beliefs

What We Believe

Diagnosis before treatment

We don't recommend a fix before confirming what's broken. It sounds obvious. It's also the most skipped step in healthcare consulting and branding.

Healthcare, deliberately, for now

Our framework was built from cross-industry case work, but the practice is healthcare-only for now. The regulatory environment and the specific ways operations break in healthcare are different enough from other industries that we'd rather go deep here before going wide.

Evidence over adjectives

We'd rather tell you what a workflow audit found than tell you we're innovative. If a claim in our materials can't be traced to something we did, it doesn't belong in them.
